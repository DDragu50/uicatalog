# How to use the app

## Find a component

- Open a group in the menu on the left — **Basic components** or **Complex components** — and choose a component.
- Or open **Search** (in the menu, or with the magnifying glass of the header search): type a word — it looks in names, keywords, groups and definitions, and every word must match — or choose a keyword chip. Below the results, **All components** shows every group with each component's definition and keywords.
- Basic components use at most one other component; complex components use two or more.

## Read a component page

- **Keywords** under the title describe the component; one of them tells how many other components it uses.
- **Uses** and **Used by** link to the components it is built from and to the components built with it. The paragraph after them is the component's definition.
- **The live example** shows the component with the showcase look (`client/showcase-styling`, the components' original look; the library itself now carries the app look). Below it, **Output** lists the configuration that was sent, the instance members, the public state, the last callbacks and any developer messages.
- **States** (Button, for example) shows every type in every state: default, hover, pressed, keyboard focus and disabled.
- **Features** and **Dependencies** come from the component's README. The button at the end (e.g. **buttonREADME.md**) opens the whole README on its own page: its file name, its place in the library (`source/components/…`), **Return to the component**, **Download .md** and **Download .pdf** (the print dialog, where you choose "Save as PDF"), and on the right a **Contents** list of all its headings.

## Try variants

- On wide screens the **Variants** column is on the right. Its double-arrow button opens (and closes) the component's options. Change them and choose **Render** to see the result in the live example; the Output shows what was sent.
- On narrow screens, **Show variants** opens the same options on a page of their own.
- Your choices are kept while the browser tab is open.

## Contact

Use **Contact** in the menu to send a question or a suggestion.
